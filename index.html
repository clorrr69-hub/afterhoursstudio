About

Precision noise, human touch.

I work with indie and electronic artists to craft mixes and production that feel raw, dynamic, and alive. With my background in Odonis Odonis, I specialize in heavy textures, detailed sound design, and bold sonic choices that elevate your music without losing its heart.

From jagged indie riffs to pulsating electronic rhythms, I specialize in crafting powerful, emotionally charged sound. With experience honed through years producing and performing with Odonis Odonis, I offer mixing, mastering, sound design, and full production for artists who want something distinctive and sonically fearless.
